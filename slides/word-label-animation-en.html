About this video

This animation explains the word-labelled method from the paper Finite graph certificates for composite terms in rational floor sequences by Yuri Odagiri (Section 4 and Section 5.3): the proof that for every real ξ > 0, infinitely many terms of ⌊ξ(5/2)n⌋ are divisible by one of 2, 3, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, 29, 31, and hence composite. It continues the story of the one-step method video.

The graphs are not pictures of pre-computed results: each time it opens, the page runs stages 0 to 6 of the word-labelled computation (36,843 vertices at stage 6), and its counts agree with Table 6 of the paper. The numbers shown for stages 7 to 9, and the 210-letter word, come from the full computation reported there.
