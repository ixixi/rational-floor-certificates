About this video

This animation explains Sections 6 to 8, 10 and 11 and Appendices B to E of the paper Finite graph certificates for composite terms in rational floor sequences by Yuri Odagiri: the waiting-time theorem for ⌊ξ(7/5)n⌋ (Theorem 3); the scope of the certificate method and its obstructions (the six operations, pseudo-orbits, the large-numerator and two-carry obstructions, the external-prime budget and the normal form); and the further results and open problems (recurrent carries, finite heights for 6/5 and 9/7, and the halting program for (6t+3)/(6t+1)). It follows the one-step method and word-labelled method videos.

The worst-case waiting times shown were found for this video by an exact interval search over every ξ with 14 ≤ ⌊ξ⌋ < 107; the longest wait there is 13 steps, far below the proved bound. The counts of the 491,520-vertex rank-certificate graph were recomputed and agree with Table 7 of the paper. The step counts of the halting program for 9/7 (every prime start up to 107 stops within 7 steps) were also computed for this video and are not in the paper. Each time it opens, the page re-checks the record orbit it shows in exact arithmetic.
