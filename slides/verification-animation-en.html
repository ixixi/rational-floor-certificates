About this video

This animation explains Section 9 and Appendix A of the paper Finite graph certificates for composite terms in rational floor sequences by Yuri Odagiri, together with its supplement: the eight obligations of the finite computations, the two independent implementations and their element-by-element comparison, the Lean formalization (the kernel-checked certificate for 7/5, and the word-labelled pipeline for 5/2 accepted with native_decide), what the formalization does not cover, and how to reproduce everything. It follows the one-step method, word-labelled method and waiting times, limits and open problems videos.

The Lean code on screen is quoted verbatim from supplement/lean. The axiom dependencies shown are those reported in the paper; the repository does not contain a transcript of the #print axioms output. As the paper’s acknowledgments state, the mathematics, the programs and the Lean formalization were produced by AI systems, and the paper has not been independently reviewed by human mathematicians; the author asks readers to check it.

The narration was generated with Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and plays in sync with the captions.
